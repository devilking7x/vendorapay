## Title
Vendora Pay AI

## Tagline
Sell anything, anywhere — just describe it. AI builds your storefront, PayPal handles the money, an agent closes the sale.

## Description

**The problem:** Freelancers and creators worldwide lose sales to friction — building a storefront takes days, checkout integration takes longer, and follow-ups on unpaid invoices never happen.

**The solution:** Vendora Pay AI is a conversational storefront builder with a **real PayPal integration** and an **agentic AI salesperson** that runs the whole sale autonomously.

### What it does
- 🎙️ **Voice-to-storefront** — describe your product out loud, AI builds the page
- 💬 **AI page builder** — chat your way to a complete, beautiful storefront
- 💳 **Real PayPal checkout** — Orders API, live sandbox-verified
- 🧾 **Smart invoices** — AI-drafted, PayPal Invoicing API
- 🔁 **Subscriptions** — recurring billing via PayPal
- 🤖 **Agentic salesperson** *(the star)* — the AI chats with buyers, negotiates **within seller-set bounds**, sends invoices, and follows up until paid. It **never charges without seller confirmation** — guardrails are hard constraints, not suggestions
- 🛡️ **AI dispute helper** — drafts professional dispute responses
- 🎟️ **Discount coupons** — single-use enforced, consumed only on successful capture
- 📊 **Analytics dashboard** — views, revenue, conversion, top products

### The agentic commerce loop
Describe → AI builds page → buyer chats → agent negotiates → invoice sent → AI follows up → paid. The seller sets the limits; the agent does the work. Every decision is logged with its reasoning.

### Built with
PayPal Orders/Invoicing/Subscriptions APIs, NVIDIA Nemotron (via Nebius), Node.js, TypeScript, Express, React, Vite, Tailwind CSS
